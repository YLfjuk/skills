# Comparison content

Help readers choose a product for their situation. State whose perspective the page represents and the audience it serves.

## Compare fairly

Credit competitors' strengths, acknowledge your product's gaps, and explain who should choose each option. Describe shipped capabilities separately from roadmap promises. If you cannot explain a competitor's appeal, research further before recommending against it.

Choose criteria from the reader's requirements. Apply the same definitions across products; avoid selecting only features that favor the publisher.

## Make claims checkable

Use current primary sources for consequential claims. Verify both your product and competitors. Record evidence for corrections and updates.

For each recommendation, connect the deciding criterion to a concrete requirement: for example, a deployment restriction can rule out an otherwise capable tool. Distinguish measured findings from judgment.

Do not turn "not found in the docs" into "not supported." Mark uncertainty and distinguish native support, integrations, partial support, and plan restrictions where they affect the decision. A checkmark alone can hide those differences.

For price comparisons, specify the workload, billing period, currency, and plan assumptions. Do not compare a base subscription with another product's estimated total cost as if they covered the same usage.

## Keep corrections maintainable

When facts recur across pages, reuse canonical product data and consistent feature definitions. Check prose as well as tables when facts change. Verify competitor corrections and apply them wherever the claim appears.

Use the project's existing maintenance process. Suggest monitoring only when repeated drift warrants it; do not introduce scheduled automation for a single article. If monitoring exists, have it surface material changes with sources and proposed edits for review.

## Review the decision

Could a reader choose a different product using the evidence presented? Would the recommendation change under a plausible different requirement? Make those conditions explicit. Remove rankings that have no stated basis.
